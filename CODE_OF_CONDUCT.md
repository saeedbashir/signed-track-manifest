# Code of conduct

This project is about access for Deaf people and people who sign. The people it
is for — and the interpreters whose work it carries — are part of the
conversation about it, and many of them use English as a second language. Write
with that in mind.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, in issues, pull requests, reviews and commit messages.
- Criticise work, not people. Say what is wrong and, where you can, what would be right.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding someone meant harm.
- Plain language over jargon, especially when explaining a decision.
- Credit the work of others, including the interpreters in any footage discussed.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks, in public or in private.
- Demeaning language about deafness, disability, sign languages or signers —
  including treating a sign language as a lesser form of a spoken one.
- Sharing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Publishing identifiable footage or images of an interpreter, or of Deaf people,
  in an issue or pull request without a licence to do so.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or continuing after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in every space the project runs — the repository, its issues and
pull requests, and any discussion the maintainers host — and to anyone acting as
the project's representative elsewhere.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer, Saeed Bashir, at **saeed.aik@gmail.com**, with
"signed-track-manifest conduct" in the subject. Reports are read by the
maintainer only and kept confidential; you will get a reply within a week. If
the report concerns the maintainer, say so, and it will be passed unread to
GitHub's own reporting route instead.

## What happens

The maintainer decides, in proportion to what happened: a private word, a public
note on the thread, removal of the content, or, for serious or repeated
behaviour, a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. The person
reported is told what was decided and why, unless doing so would put the
reporter at risk.

This code draws on the Contributor Covenant (https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1.
